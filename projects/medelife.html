The Journey & Objective

MEDeLIFE is a project defined by its continuous evolution. What initially started as an entry for the Hult Prize 2025 (where it secured Runner-up) was expanded and rigorously developed over 3 months into my Final Year Project—earning recognition as one of the best projects of the semester. Driven by this success, I refined the platform and submitted it to the Hult Prize 2026, officially shortlisting for Stage 2 (Nationals).

The core objective was to build a comprehensive, secure healthcare ecosystem that seamlessly bridged the communication and data gaps between patients, doctors, and pharmacies.

Key Features & Architecture

Tri-Role Architecture

Engineered three isolated but interconnected dashboards. Patients can book appointments and manage health data, Doctors manage schedules and patient diagnostics, and Pharmacies handle prescriptions.

Secure Medical Records

Developed a dual-upload system. Patients can upload their personal medical history. During appointments, doctors can view these files alongside docs uploaded by previous doctors, and append new files into secure, role-separated folders.

Verified Medical Community

Built an integrated social forum where any user can ask questions or share opinions. To combat medical misinformation, only verified Doctor accounts have the authorization to provide official answers and publish health articles.
