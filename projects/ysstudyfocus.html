The Objective

YSStudyFocus was my very first professional project. The primary objective was to build a comprehensive, high-converting digital presence for an education agency facilitating international student recruitment in Malaysia. It required bridging the gap between an authoritative corporate aesthetic and an accessible, user-friendly interface for prospective students.

Key Features & Architecture

Modern Glassmorphism UI

Engineered a highly visual frontend using glassmorphism principles to provide depth and hierarchy, moving away from flat, lifeless corporate templates.

End-to-End Recruitment Funnel

Integrated clear call-to-actions and lead capture systems allowing the agency to streamline student inquiries effortlessly without intrusive barriers.

Responsive & Performant

Optimized for mobile-first indexing, ensuring students browsing from various global regions and network speeds experience smooth animations without lag.
