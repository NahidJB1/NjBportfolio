The Objective

One Step is a large-scale agency operating across 12 different countries, recruiting both international students and foreign workers. The challenge was to design and develop a complex, data-heavy platform within a strict 2-week timeline. It required an intuitive architecture so users could easily filter through massive amounts of university, program, and geographical data without feeling overwhelmed.

Key Features & Architecture

Smart Subject Search Engine

Developed a dynamic search flow: users search for a specific subject, instantly view all universities offering it, and can click straight through to the detailed program requirements.

Comprehensive Program Directories

Integrated specific "Programs" modules for 14 top Malaysian universities (and 12 other countries). This expands to show exhaustive course details including duration, yearly fees, and total fees.

Dedicated Institution Hubs

Engineered dynamically generated sub-pages for each university and country. These hubs house rich media, including location data, intake periods, institutional specialties, and photo galleries.
