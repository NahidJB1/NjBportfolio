The Problem & Objective

Fake news travels at the speed of panic. Existing fact-checking tools like Snopes require days for human review, and manual Google searches take too much effort in the moment of consumption[cite: 140]. Our objective was clear: design and develop an instant AI-powered fact-checking platform that gives users the truth in under 15 seconds — verified forever on the blockchain[cite: 140].

Conceived and built entirely within a 30-day university hackathon[cite: 140], I led the system architecture, full-stack development, and feature planning, collaborating with my teammate who handled the UI. The platform won 1st place at the CityU FusionX Hackathon, judged by industry cybersecurity professionals[cite: 137, 140].

System Architecture & Features

AI Claim-by-Claim Breakdown

Instead of a simple binary verdict, I engineered the backend to break down articles claim-by-claim using AI[cite: 140]. It assigns a three-tier verdict (Real, Fake, Suspicious) and cross-references data against trusted global sources, showing the exact evidence[cite: 140].

Immutable Blockchain Records

To ensure absolute trust and transparency, I integrated blockchain technology. Every verification result is permanently stored on the Polygon network, generating a tamper-proof, public transaction hash that cannot be altered or deleted[cite: 140].

Live Misinformation Heatmap

Developed a dynamic, global heatmap that aggregates user submissions to display color-coded live dots[cite: 140]. This tracks where specific fake news topics (Health, Politics, War) are actively spreading, turning a personal tool into a public service[cite: 140].
